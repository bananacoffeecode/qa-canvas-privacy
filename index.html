QA canvas · Chrome extension

Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026

In short

  • QA canvas has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We never receive your data.
  • Everything you capture stays in your browser until you send it.
  • When you send something, it goes straight from your browser to the issue tracker you connected: ClickUp, Jira or Linear. Nowhere else.

What QA canvas handles

  • Captures you make: screenshots, tab recordings, written notes, and any image or video you add from your device.
  • Page details, only when you capture: the page’s address and title, your browser and operating system, window and screen size, time zone, browser language and the time. You choose which of these are added to an issue.
  • Your voice, only if you turn the microphone on: it’s recorded as part of a tab recording and nothing else.
  • Your tracker API key: the ClickUp token, Jira email and API token, or Linear API key you enter, plus the link to the task or board you send to.

Where it’s kept

All of it is stored locally in your browser, in the extension’s own storage on your device. It is not synced to any server. Captures stay until you delete them. Your API keys stay until you disconnect the tracker or remove the extension.

When it leaves your browser

Only when you choose to send a capture (Add, Send or Send all). QA canvas then creates the task or issue, with your note, the details you chose and the attachment, directly in the tracker you connected, using your API key:

TrackerWhere the data goes
ClickUpapi.clickup.com
Jirayour Jira Cloud site (yourteam.atlassian.net)
Linearapi.linear.app, and Linear’s file storage for screenshots and recordings

Once there, it’s handled under that company’s own privacy policy and your workspace’s settings.

What QA canvas never does

  • Send your data to us or to anyone other than the tracker you chose.
  • Sell, rent or share your data, or use it for advertising.
  • Track your browsing. It only looks at a page when you capture it.
  • Load code from the internet. Everything it runs ships inside the extension.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a capture with its Delete button, or everything with Settings → Delete all.
  • Remove a tracker’s key with Set up → Disconnect.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.
  • Issues you already sent live in your tracker. Delete them there.

Changes and contact
